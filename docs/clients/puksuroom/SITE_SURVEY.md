## Evidence and acceptance

Start with an empty-table view and controlled ball layouts, only after the necessary approvals. Record visible-ball detection, position stability, false detections, occlusion recovery and failure confidence against manual observations. Preserve raw counts and test conditions rather than advertising an unmeasured accuracy percentage.

Agree thresholds and test duration after the initial camera benchmark. A trial must not decide match outcomes: human scoring remains authoritative. Pause or remove the prototype if it interferes with play, creates a safety concern or cannot respect the agreed privacy boundaries.

No automatic scoring, CueScore integration, unattended operation, revenue uplift or mechanical reracking is promised by this survey.

## Candidate-to-reference gate

A public case study may be proposed only after the venue approves participation, the relevant data/image rights are addressed, and there are actual results to report. Confirm the public wording, approved photos and removal contact before publication. A completed private survey alone does not authorize publication.
